Who we are

This site runs %SITE_NAME%, an engine for multiplayer role-playing games. The people who run this site decide what it does with your information, and are responsible for it.

For questions about this policy, or to ask about your information, email %VITE_PRIVACY_CONTACT%.

What we keep

Your account

  • Your username, which is unique and lets other players find you, and your display name, which needn't be your real name.
  • Your email address, used to log in and to send you emails about your account.
  • If you have a local account, your password, kept only in a scrambled form that can't be turned back into the password (a one-way hash: Argon2id for passwords and recovery codes, and a keyed hash, HMAC, for the codes and addresses below).
  • If you log in with Google or Discord, the ID and email address of that account, which they send us when you log in.
  • If you turn on two-factor authentication, the secret your authenticator app shares with us, and your recovery codes, kept scrambled like a password.

Your profile

Anything you choose to fill in on your profile: a photo, your pronouns, your location and time zone, your language, links to your social media accounts, and which of your characters others can see. Only your username and display name are required.

What you play and build

  • Your characters, their items and progress, the groups they are in, and the parts of each universe your groups have explored.
  • The universes you create or help edit, and what you upload to them, such as tile images.
  • Chat isn't saved. The game server keeps a lobby's most recent messages in memory, so players who arrive can read them, until the lobby closes or the server restarts.

Security records

  • A record for each browser you are logged in on, so you stay logged in, and so you can be logged out everywhere.
  • The codes we email you, to register, reset your password or change your email address, kept only in a scrambled form, and only until they are used or expire 15 minutes later.
  • To stop people guessing codes and passwords, counts of the attempts made for each email address and from each network, with the addresses kept only in a scrambled form, and only while a limit still needs them.
  • A log of logins, failed logins and changes to your account's security, such as a new password, with your network in a scrambled form. It never holds passwords or codes, and is kept for 90 days.

What we don't do

We don't show ads, don't use analytics or tracking services, and never sell your information or share it with anyone for their own use. We only email you about your account, never to sell you something.

Cookies and browser storage

We store only what the site needs to work, so we don't ask before storing it. There are no cookies from other sites.

  • A cookie that keeps you logged in. It ends when you log out, after an hour without use, or when you close your browser; with "Remember me" checked, after 30 days without use.
  • After you enter a code we emailed you, a cookie that lets that browser take the next step, such as choosing a password, for 10 minutes.
  • The language you choose at the bottom of the page.
  • The character each tab last played, so reloading the tab plays them again, until the tab closes.

Who can see it

  • Other players see your username, display name and the profile fields you fill in, the characters you let them see, and what your characters say and do in the game. Players in a group with you can always reach your profile.
  • The editors of a universe can see the characters played in it.
  • The people who run this site can see what we keep, and look at it only to run the site, keep it safe and deal with reports of abuse.
  • Other players never see your email address.

Services we use

  • Companies that host our database, game server and uploaded files, and that send our emails. They handle your information only on our instructions, and may store it in another country than yours.
  • Google or Discord, if you log in with them. They learn that you logged in here, under their own privacy policies.
  • When you choose a password, we check it against Have I Been Pwned's list of passwords leaked from other sites. We send it only the first 5 characters of a scrambled form of your password, which can't reveal the password.

Why we keep it

Privacy laws such as the GDPR ask us to name the legal basis for each use of your information:

  • Performance of a contract: your account, your characters and groups, the universes you build, and the emails about your account, which we need to give you the account, the game and the universe editor you asked for.
  • Consent: your profile's optional fields, which you chose to share. You can withdraw your consent by removing them whenever you like.
  • Legitimate interests: the security records and the check of new passwords against leaked ones, which keep your account and the site safe and stop cheating and abuse.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile and characters, until you delete them.
  • A character you delete, until the days the site waits before deleting characters have passed.
  • Security records as long as the list above says: the log for 90 days, codes for 15 minutes, and counts only while a limit needs them.
  • Deleted information can stay in our backups for a short time, until they are replaced.

Deleting your account deletes your profile, your characters and your security records. Groups led by one of your characters disband. For each universe you are the only creator of, you choose whether to hand it to another of its editors or delete it; what you built in universes others edit stays part of them.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your information on your profile.
  • Download everything we keep about you, as one file, from your profile.
  • Delete your account from your profile.

Children

You must be at least 13 to have an account, or older where the law of your country sets a higher age for agreeing to this policy yourself. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

Keeping it safe

Everything you send the site is encrypted on its way. Passwords and codes are only kept scrambled, and you can turn on two-factor authentication. If your information is ever exposed, we will tell you.

Changes to this policy

The date at the top of this page shows when this policy last changed. If it changes in a way that matters to you, we will email you before the change takes effect.
