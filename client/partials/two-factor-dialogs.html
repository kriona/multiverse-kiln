Turn on two-factor authentication

With two-factor authentication, logging in also asks for a code from an app on your phone, so someone who learns your password still can't get in. Every other browser logged in to your account is logged out when it turns on. First, enter your password.

Scan this QR code with an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Aegis or 2FAS. Then enter the 6-digit code the app shows.
