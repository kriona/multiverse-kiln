Coins

Coin Name Abbreviation

What the universe calls each coin, and what amounts of it are written with, like the gp in 2gp. Write names as they'd read mid-sentence, like gold pieces. Leave a field empty to use the default name. Abbreviations can't have spaces, digits or commas, and each coin needs its own abbreviation. Name and abbreviation are translated on the Translations tab.

Difficulty

The difficulty levels a new group chooses from. Each level makes mobs harder or easier, and can make death permanent.

                                  Start a Draft

                                  A draft is a copy of the whole universe: its settings, races, classes, items, tilesets, areas and their cells, and translations. Players keep playing the live version and won't see draft changes until they are applied.

                                  When the draft is ready, a creator applies it with Apply Draft at the top of the editor, and players see everything changed in it at once. Discard Draft deletes it instead, leaving the live version as it is.

                                  ⚠️ Applying the draft overwrites the entire universe with it, including any changes saved to the live version while the draft is open. Applying can't be undone.
