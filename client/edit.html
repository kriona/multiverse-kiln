Ruleset:

Settings for

Rolled scores can be rearranged among the abilities. Changing this doesn't change characters that already exist.

Every ability score starts at 10, and each point raises one by 1, up to 18 with the race's and theme's bonuses. Changing this doesn't change characters that already exist.

The game doesn't use sleep, weight or ammunition yet. These are kept for when it does.

The lobby is where everyone in the universe meets before joining a group. It is an area like any other, but isn't listed on the Areas tab.

Actions take no time in the lobby, so its time of day follows this instead of the universe's passage of time.

Tiles don't block or shorten anyone's view in the lobby, regardless of the tile's vision cost. Characters see as far as in full daylight at any time of day.

      Indoors or outdoors

      A lit area is as light as full daylight at any time of day, and a dark one as dark as night. Outdoor areas follow the time of day.

      An area whose passage of time is faerie can't loop.

      Map

      Tool

      Drag with the right mouse button to look around, and zoom by holding Ctrl and turning the mouse wheel. Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y redoes. With the Links tool, click a cell to add or change its link.

      With the map focused, the arrow keys move the cursor, 10 cells at a time with Shift. Space or Enter paints, or with the Links tool opens the cell's link; hold Space while moving to keep painting. + and - zoom, and Home goes back to the center cell.
