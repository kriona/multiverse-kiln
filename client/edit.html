Rolled scores can be rearranged among the abilities. Changing this doesn't change characters that already exist.

The game doesn't use sleep, weight or ammunition yet. These are kept for when it does.

The lobby is where everyone in the universe meets before joining a group. It is a zone like any other, but isn't listed on the Zones tab.

      Map

      Tool

      Drag with the right mouse button to look around, and zoom with the mouse wheel. Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y redoes. With the Links tool, click a cell to add or change its link.

      With the map focused, the arrow keys move the cursor, 10 cells at a time with Shift. Space or Enter paints, or with the Links tool opens the cell's link; hold Space while moving to keep painting. + and - zoom, and Home goes back to the center cell.
